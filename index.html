INAV Disarm Hold Firmware

For goggles that stop recording at disarm, before the stats screen appears.

Unofficial and experimental. Not made or supported by the INAV team.

These are builds of official INAV source with one added setting. The feature was tested on a few boards; most of these firmware files have never been run on hardware. Test with props off first. Report problems with these builds on the Discord below, not to INAV.

Install

  1. Make sure your INAV 9 craft is fully set up and flying properly.
  2. Make a full diff all backup in the CLI. Run version too and note your exact target.
  3. Download the firmware for your FC here. Nothing is downloaded until you press the button.
  4. In Firmware Flasher choose Load Firmware [Local] and flash. Changing INAV version: use full chip erase.
  5. Restore your craft parameters: paste your diff all into the CLI and save.
  6. Set in the CLI:
    set osd_msp_disarm_hold = 5
save
    0 to 7 seconds. 0 is off, and is the default.
  7. Bench test with props off: arm or force arm, let the motors run for 10 seconds or more, then disarm. The motors must stop at once while the goggles keep recording.
  8. Enjoy the new feature.

What it changes

  • For the set time after a disarm, status replies on the MSP DisplayPort port still say armed. That is what keeps the goggles recording.
  • The flight controller disarms immediately. Other MSP ports, MAVLink and the Configurator see the real state.
  • To go back, flash official INAV from the Configurator.
